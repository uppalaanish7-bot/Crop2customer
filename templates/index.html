HELP & TRANSPARENCY

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about buying and selling on Crop2Costumer.

How does payment work if there is no online payment gateway?

Crop2Costumer is a direct order-placement marketplace. Customers select a crop, enter their delivery address, and confirm their order without entering card or UPI details online. Payment is settled directly between the buyer and seller outside the platform upon dispatch or doorstep delivery.

Can I use the same phone number for both Buyer and Seller accounts?

Yes. Buyer and Seller roles are strictly separated with independent permissions and dashboards. You can sign in as a Seller to manage your farm listings, or sign in as a Buyer to purchase crops from other farmers (note: you cannot purchase your own listing).

How does phone OTP login work in local development vs. production?

When an SMS provider (Twilio or Fast2SMS) is configured via environment variables, the server dispatches a real SMS code. When running in local development/demo mode without external SMS credentials, the interface clearly indicates Demo Mode and accepts the common development OTP 1234.

What happens when two buyers try to order the last available stock at the same time?

Every order runs inside an atomic SQLite BEGIN IMMEDIATE database transaction on the server. Stock is verified and decremented atomically so overselling cannot occur.

Can a buyer cancel an order after placing it?

Yes. Buyers can cancel an order directly from their “My Orders” dashboard while the order is in Order Placed or Accepted status, which automatically restores the reserved crop quantity back to the seller's listing.

Where are uploaded crop photos and records stored?

Structured records (users, listings, stock, orders, addresses) are stored in data/marketplace.db (SQLite), and uploaded crop images are validated, optimized via Pillow, and stored persistently in uploads/.
